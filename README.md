# Andi & Emma

A narrative-driven 2D platformer about love, survival, and difficult choices at the very start of the end of the world.

## High Concept

Set in the first days of a post-apocalyptic world, the game follows Andi as he navigates a collapsing city, unstable underground systems, and increasingly desperate survivors. 

## Genre

- 2D story-rich platformer
- Light survival systems
- Exploration and environmental storytelling

## Story Premise

An unexplained chain reaction begins.
Infrastructure fails.
Public messages conflict.
People disappear below ground.

Andi and Emma are separated during the first wave of chaos. Armed with only fragments of information and a few personal items, Andi and Emma move through districts that are transforming by the hour. As they get closer to each other, they discover the disaster may not be purely natural, and survival may depend on understanding the system behind it.

## Tone and Themes

- urgent, grounded, and intimate
- trust vs control
- truth vs official narrative
- hope as an act of resistance

## Gameplay Loop (Example)

1. Explore a district and identify a route forward.
2. Platform through hazards and scavenging opportunities.
3. Manage short-term survival needs.
4. Encounter survivors and make story choices.
5. Reach a checkpoint/safe room and unlock the next lead on Emma.

## Visual Direction

- layered 2D environments with decaying urban + industrial underground spaces
- strong contrast between "normal world" remnants and sudden collapse
- environmental cues that show escalating instability over time

## Audio Direction

- distant alarms, failing machinery, emergency broadcasts
- minimalist score focused on tension and emotional release
- silence used intentionally in abandoned spaces

## MVP Scope

- 1 playable chapter (30-60 minutes)
- movement set: run, jump, wall slide, ledge grab
- 2-3 hazard types
- basic inventory/resource system
- 3-5 narrative choice moments
- chapter-ending reunion cliffhanger

## Long-Term Vision

- multi-chapter campaign following Andi and Emma from separation to reunion
- branching story outcomes based on trust and survival choices
- deeper worldbuilding around the origin of the collapse and hidden underground governance
